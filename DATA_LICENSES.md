## Mozilla Common Voice

The source dataset is **Common Voice Scripted Speech 26.0 — Ibibio (`ibb`)**,
dataset ID `cmqiohial00zgnq07ybhhu7ni`, published under **CC0-1.0**.

The source archive and extracted audio are not distributed in this repository.
Their version, licence, and checksum are recorded for reproducibility. CC0 does
not remove privacy, publicity, personality, or similar rights that may apply to
recorded voices.

## Model checkpoints

No model-weight licence is granted by the repository's MIT licence. Each model
release must declare its own licence after all pretrained components and their
terms have been reviewed. Do not publish checkpoints before the required data,
technical, and native-speaker evaluations are complete.
